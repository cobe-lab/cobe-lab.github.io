# COBE Lab equipment documentation

This is Cognition and Behavior Lab's quick guides to use our equipment.

Here is a condensed list of available equipment at Cognition and Behavior Lab.

- [EyeLink 1000 eye tracker](/guides/eye-tracking/eyelink1000)
- [Tobii T60 XL eye tracker](/guides/eye-tracking/tobii)
- Aurora eye tracking laptop kits*
- HP Reverb G2 Omnicept Edition
- Meta Quest Pro
- Oculus Quest 2 and 3
- HTC Vive + eye tracking add on
- [BIOPAC systems](/guides/physiological/Biopack_guide)
- [Senstream Research Rings](/guides/physiological/resrings)
- Enobio EEG
- Brain Products EEG
- [mBrainTrain EEG](/guides/EEG/COBE-EEG-Lab)
- [Qualisys Motion Capture system](/guides/motion_capture/motion_capture)

See a full list of equipment and other resources [here](https://bss.au.dk/en/cognition-and-behavior-lab/for-researchers/labs-equipment).

If you have any comments or suggestions for improvements, please let the lab manager know at cobelab@au.dk.

*Unfortunately, we do not have the funding for the licenses for the software necessary to use our Aurora eye trackers at the moment. 
If you wish to use our Aurora eye trackers and have funding to pay for a license, please send an email to the lab manager at cobelab@au.dk.
