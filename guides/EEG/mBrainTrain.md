# mBrainTrain EEG system
This is a quick guide for how to use our wireless EEG system from [*mBrainTrain*](https://mbraintrain.com/smarting-pro-line/). The [*Smarting Pro EEG wireless amplifier*](https://mbraintrain.com/smarting-pro-line/) from mBrainTrain has 32 channels and
a recording software, Smarting Pro Streamer, which is fully integrated with Lab Streaming Layer which allows for data synchronization over a local network.
The EEG system from mBrainTrain also offers hyperscanning which is suitable for studies with more than one participant. 
COBE Lab has two wireless EEG amplifiers from mBrainTrain and two 32 channel caps. 

The wireless system allows you to collect high quality outside of the lab. COBE Lab also has a dedicated space for EEG data collection. 
The space has two recording rooms, four computers in a pc rack, and a control room with four monitors. 

**This guide includes:**

1. How to prepare for data collection
2. How to mount the cap
3. How to wash the cap

## How to mount the cap

1. Turn on the recording software and select the mounting setting
2. Before filling the cap, click on streaming in the recording software
3. Fill the ground electrode, the reference electrode and a third electrode with gel. To properly fill each electrode, you should: 1) move the hair slightly with the applicator, 2) add gel, and 3) move the applicator around on the head
4. While filling the electrodes with gel keep an eye on the impedance values (and colours) in the recording software. The impedance should go below 10 (light green) for all electrodes
5. Once you have filled the three electrodes, turn of streaming
6. Under impedance measurement, change to all electrodes
7. Turn streaming back on
8. Fill the rest of the electrodes. They should also all be green. If it is not possible to achieve, yellow is also fine.
9. Turn impedance measurement off and stop streaming
10. Turn streaming on one more time. You can confirm from the visualisation whether the data quality is good. The visualisation is processed, only the raw data is stored.

## How to wash the cap
Remove the amplifier from its socket and place it inside the plastic bag dedicated for washing the cap. Wash the cap in the sink in the control room using lukewarm water. 
Be careful not to get too much water on the amplifier even though it is inside the plastic bag. The bag is splash proof not water proof. Use the mascara brushes to clean the electrodes. 

Once clean, leave the cap to dry on a towel beside the sink. Only store the equipment when it is completely dry.
