This guide briefly explains the EEG lab setup at COBE Lab. 

The EEG lab two lab rooms. The small lab room is suitable for testing a single participant. The large lab room is suitable for testing up to x participants, 
depending on your study design.

The lab also features a control room with four screens connected to four computers in a pc rack in a anteroom. From the control room, you can manage data collection and 
view the participant's screen. The control room is also equipped with a camera stream hosted on a PC, where you can supervise the participant in the lab room. The control room also 
features a speaker system from which you can speak to the participant.
